# Privacy Policy — Email Cleaning
   This app accesses only the Gmail account of its owner. Email metadata (sender,
   subject, date, labels, short snippet) is processed on the owner's local computer
   to label and clean the inbox. Metadata may be sent to the Anthropic API for
   classification. No data is sold, shared with other parties, or used for any other
   purpose. Contact: <jayarseneault@gmail.com>
